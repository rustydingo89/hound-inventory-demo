# Inventory app demo

An offline-capable inventory search app demo.

Demo with fictional distributors, stock and cost. Product names and MSRPs are for illustration; brands are trademarks of their owners; not affiliated.
MSRPs are rounded from public manufacturer sources and are illustrative, not a price list. The store, distributors,
stock, dealer/landed costs, SKUs, part numbers and UPCs are invented.
Nothing is sent anywhere; lists and settings are saved only in your browser.

Open the site, or on a phone use "Add to Home Screen" to install it and use it offline.

Demo for evaluation only; do not redistribute.
